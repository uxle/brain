# ─── PHYSICS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: physics
DEFINITION: the natural science that studies matter, energy, force, and motion and how they interact

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: force
DEFINITION: a push or pull on an object measured in newtons that can change its velocity or shape
FORMULA: F = m * a

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: mass
DEFINITION: the amount of matter in an object measured in kilograms

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: weight
DEFINITION: the gravitational force acting on an object equal to mass times gravitational acceleration
FORMULA: W = m * g

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: velocity
DEFINITION: the speed of an object in a given direction measured in meters per second

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: acceleration
DEFINITION: the rate of change of velocity over time measured in meters per second squared
FORMULA: a = (v - u) / t

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: speed
DEFINITION: the distance traveled per unit of time without regard to direction
FORMULA: speed = distance / time

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: distance
DEFINITION: the total length of the path traveled by an object measured in meters
FORMULA: distance = speed * time

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: momentum
DEFINITION: the product of an objects mass and velocity representing its quantity of motion
FORMULA: p = m * v

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: energy
DEFINITION: the capacity to do work or cause change measured in joules

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: kinetic_energy
DEFINITION: the energy an object possesses due to its motion
FORMULA: KE = 0.5 * m * v^2

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: potential_energy
DEFINITION: the energy stored in an object due to its position or configuration
FORMULA: PE = m * g * h

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: work
DEFINITION: the transfer of energy when a force moves an object through a distance
FORMULA: W = F * d * cos(theta)

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: power
DEFINITION: the rate at which work is done or energy is transferred measured in watts
FORMULA: P = W / t

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: watt
DEFINITION: the SI unit of power equal to one joule per second

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: joule
DEFINITION: the SI unit of energy equal to the work done by a force of one newton over one meter

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: newton
DEFINITION: the SI unit of force required to accelerate one kilogram by one meter per second squared

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: gravity
DEFINITION: the fundamental force of attraction between all objects with mass
FORMULA: F = G * m1 * m2 / r^2

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: gravitational_acceleration
DEFINITION: the acceleration due to gravity near Earths surface approximately 9.81 meters per second squared

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: newtons_first_law
DEFINITION: an object at rest stays at rest and an object in motion stays in motion unless acted upon by an external force

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: newtons_second_law
DEFINITION: the acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force and inversely proportional to its mass
FORMULA: F = m * a

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: newtons_third_law
DEFINITION: for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: friction
DEFINITION: the force that opposes the relative motion of two surfaces in contact

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: pressure
DEFINITION: the force applied per unit area measured in pascals
FORMULA: P = F / A

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: pascal
DEFINITION: the SI unit of pressure equal to one newton per square meter

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: density
DEFINITION: the mass per unit volume of a substance measured in kilograms per cubic meter
FORMULA: density = mass / volume

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: buoyancy
DEFINITION: the upward force exerted on an object submerged in a fluid equal to the weight of displaced fluid

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: archimedes_principle
DEFINITION: an object immersed in a fluid experiences an upward buoyant force equal to the weight of the fluid it displaces

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: temperature
DEFINITION: a measure of the average kinetic energy of particles in a substance

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: heat
DEFINITION: the transfer of thermal energy from a hotter object to a cooler object

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: thermodynamics
DEFINITION: the branch of physics that deals with heat energy and its relationship to work and other forms of energy

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: first_law_of_thermodynamics
DEFINITION: energy cannot be created or destroyed only transformed from one form to another

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: second_law_of_thermodynamics
DEFINITION: the total entropy of an isolated system always increases over time

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: third_law_of_thermodynamics
DEFINITION: as temperature approaches absolute zero the entropy of a perfect crystal approaches zero

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: entropy
DEFINITION: a measure of the disorder or randomness in a system

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: absolute_zero
DEFINITION: the lowest possible temperature at which all molecular motion ceases equal to minus 273.15 degrees Celsius or 0 kelvin

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: wave
DEFINITION: a disturbance that transfers energy through matter or space without transferring matter

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: wavelength
DEFINITION: the distance between two consecutive crests or troughs of a wave

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: frequency
DEFINITION: the number of complete wave cycles that pass a point per second measured in hertz

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: hertz
DEFINITION: the SI unit of frequency equal to one cycle per second

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: amplitude
DEFINITION: the maximum displacement of a wave from its equilibrium position

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: sound
DEFINITION: a mechanical wave that travels through a medium by compressing and expanding particles

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: speed_of_sound
DEFINITION: the speed at which sound travels approximately 343 meters per second in air at room temperature

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: light
DEFINITION: electromagnetic radiation visible to the human eye with wavelengths between 380 and 700 nanometers

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: speed_of_light
DEFINITION: the speed at which light travels in a vacuum approximately 299792458 meters per second or about 300000 kilometers per second

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: reflection
DEFINITION: the bouncing of a wave off a surface where the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: refraction
DEFINITION: the bending of a wave as it passes from one medium to another due to a change in speed

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: diffraction
DEFINITION: the bending of waves around obstacles or through openings

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: electromagnetic_spectrum
DEFINITION: the complete range of electromagnetic radiation from radio waves to gamma rays

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: radio_waves
DEFINITION: electromagnetic waves with the longest wavelengths used for communication and broadcasting

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: microwaves
DEFINITION: electromagnetic waves with wavelengths between radio waves and infrared used in cooking and radar

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: infrared
DEFINITION: electromagnetic radiation with wavelengths longer than visible light associated with heat

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: ultraviolet
DEFINITION: electromagnetic radiation with wavelengths shorter than visible light that can cause sunburn

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: xray
DEFINITION: high energy electromagnetic radiation that can pass through soft tissue used in medical imaging

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: gamma_ray
DEFINITION: the highest energy electromagnetic radiation emitted by radioactive decay and nuclear reactions

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: electricity
DEFINITION: the flow of electric charge through a conductor

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: electric_current
DEFINITION: the rate of flow of electric charge measured in amperes
FORMULA: I = Q / t

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: voltage
DEFINITION: the electric potential difference between two points that drives current flow measured in volts
FORMULA: V = I * R

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: resistance
DEFINITION: the opposition to the flow of electric current measured in ohms
FORMULA: R = V / I

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: ohms_law
DEFINITION: the current through a conductor is directly proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance
FORMULA: V = I * R

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: circuit
DEFINITION: a closed loop path through which electric current can flow

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: series_circuit
DEFINITION: a circuit where components are connected end to end so the same current flows through each

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: parallel_circuit
DEFINITION: a circuit where components are connected across each other so the voltage is the same across each

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: coulombs_law
DEFINITION: the force between two charged particles is proportional to the product of their charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them
FORMULA: F = k * q1 * q2 / r^2

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: magnetism
DEFINITION: the force of attraction or repulsion between magnetic materials caused by moving electric charges

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: electromagnetic_induction
DEFINITION: the production of an electric current by changing the magnetic field through a conductor

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: faradays_law
DEFINITION: a changing magnetic flux through a circuit induces an electromotive force proportional to the rate of change

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: special_relativity
DEFINITION: Einsteins theory that the laws of physics are the same in all inertial reference frames and the speed of light is constant
FORMULA: E = m * c^2

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: general_relativity
DEFINITION: Einsteins theory that gravity is the curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: quantum_mechanics
DEFINITION: the branch of physics describing the behavior of matter and energy at atomic and subatomic scales

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: atom
DEFINITION: the smallest unit of an element that retains its chemical properties consisting of protons neutrons and electrons

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: proton
DEFINITION: a positively charged subatomic particle found in the nucleus of an atom

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: neutron
DEFINITION: an electrically neutral subatomic particle found in the nucleus of an atom

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: electron
DEFINITION: a negatively charged subatomic particle that orbits the nucleus of an atom

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: nucleus
DEFINITION: the dense central core of an atom containing protons and neutrons

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: radioactivity
DEFINITION: the spontaneous emission of radiation from an unstable atomic nucleus

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: half_life
DEFINITION: the time required for half of the radioactive atoms in a sample to decay

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: nuclear_fission
DEFINITION: the splitting of a heavy atomic nucleus into two lighter nuclei releasing large amounts of energy

TYPE: PHYSICS
NAME: nuclear_fusion
DEFINITION: the combining of two light atomic nuclei to form a heavier nucleus releasing enormous energy

# ─── CHEMISTRY ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: chemistry
DEFINITION: the science that studies the composition structure properties and changes of matter

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: element
DEFINITION: a pure substance made of only one type of atom that cannot be broken down by chemical means

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: compound
DEFINITION: a substance made of two or more different elements chemically bonded together in fixed proportions

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: mixture
DEFINITION: a combination of two or more substances that are not chemically bonded and can be separated by physical means

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: molecule
DEFINITION: the smallest unit of a compound consisting of two or more atoms bonded together

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: periodic_table
DEFINITION: a tabular arrangement of chemical elements organized by atomic number electron configuration and chemical properties

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: atomic_number
DEFINITION: the number of protons in the nucleus of an atom which determines the element

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: atomic_mass
DEFINITION: the weighted average mass of all isotopes of an element measured in atomic mass units

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: hydrogen
DEFINITION: the lightest and most abundant element in the universe with atomic number 1 and symbol H

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: helium
DEFINITION: a noble gas with atomic number 2 and symbol He the second lightest element

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: carbon
DEFINITION: a nonmetal element with atomic number 6 and symbol C the basis of organic chemistry and all known life

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: nitrogen
DEFINITION: a gas element with atomic number 7 and symbol N making up 78 percent of Earths atmosphere

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: oxygen
DEFINITION: a gas element with atomic number 8 and symbol O essential for respiration and making up 21 percent of Earths atmosphere

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: sodium
DEFINITION: a soft reactive alkali metal with atomic number 11 and symbol Na

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: chlorine
DEFINITION: a yellow-green halogen gas with atomic number 17 and symbol Cl used in water purification

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: iron
DEFINITION: a transition metal with atomic number 26 and symbol Fe essential for hemoglobin in blood

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: gold
DEFINITION: a precious transition metal with atomic number 79 and symbol Au known for its resistance to corrosion

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: silver
DEFINITION: a precious transition metal with atomic number 47 and symbol Ag with the highest electrical conductivity

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: copper
DEFINITION: a transition metal with atomic number 29 and symbol Cu widely used in electrical wiring

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: aluminum
DEFINITION: a lightweight silvery metal with atomic number 13 and symbol Al the most abundant metal in Earths crust

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: calcium
DEFINITION: an alkaline earth metal with atomic number 20 and symbol Ca essential for bones and teeth

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: potassium
DEFINITION: an alkali metal with atomic number 19 and symbol K essential for nerve and muscle function

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: uranium
DEFINITION: a radioactive metal with atomic number 92 and symbol U used as fuel in nuclear reactors

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: chemical_bond
DEFINITION: an attractive force that holds atoms together in molecules and compounds

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: ionic_bond
DEFINITION: a chemical bond formed by the transfer of electrons from one atom to another creating oppositely charged ions

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: covalent_bond
DEFINITION: a chemical bond formed by the sharing of electron pairs between atoms

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: metallic_bond
DEFINITION: a chemical bond formed by the sharing of free electrons among a lattice of metal atoms

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: hydrogen_bond
DEFINITION: a weak attractive force between a hydrogen atom bonded to an electronegative atom and another electronegative atom

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: chemical_reaction
DEFINITION: a process in which substances called reactants are transformed into different substances called products

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: exothermic_reaction
DEFINITION: a chemical reaction that releases heat energy to its surroundings

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: endothermic_reaction
DEFINITION: a chemical reaction that absorbs heat energy from its surroundings

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: combustion
DEFINITION: a rapid exothermic chemical reaction between a fuel and an oxidant producing heat and light

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: oxidation
DEFINITION: a chemical reaction involving the loss of electrons or an increase in oxidation state

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: reduction
DEFINITION: a chemical reaction involving the gain of electrons or a decrease in oxidation state

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: acid
DEFINITION: a substance that donates hydrogen ions in solution has a pH less than 7 and tastes sour

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: base
DEFINITION: a substance that accepts hydrogen ions in solution has a pH greater than 7 and feels slippery

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: ph
DEFINITION: a scale from 0 to 14 measuring the acidity or alkalinity of a solution where 7 is neutral

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: catalyst
DEFINITION: a substance that speeds up a chemical reaction without being consumed in the process

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: enzyme
DEFINITION: a biological catalyst usually a protein that speeds up chemical reactions in living organisms

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: solute
DEFINITION: a substance that is dissolved in a solvent to form a solution

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: solvent
DEFINITION: a substance in which a solute dissolves to form a solution typically water

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: solution
DEFINITION: a homogeneous mixture of a solute dissolved in a solvent

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: concentration
DEFINITION: the amount of solute present in a given amount of solution

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: molarity
DEFINITION: a measure of concentration defined as the number of moles of solute per liter of solution

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: mole
DEFINITION: a unit of measurement equal to 6.022 times 10 to the 23rd particles of a substance

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: avogadros_number
DEFINITION: the number of atoms or molecules in one mole of a substance equal to 6.022 times 10 to the 23rd

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: ideal_gas_law
DEFINITION: the equation of state for an ideal gas relating pressure volume amount and temperature
FORMULA: PV = nRT

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: solid
DEFINITION: a state of matter with a definite shape and volume where particles are tightly packed

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: liquid
DEFINITION: a state of matter with a definite volume but no definite shape where particles can flow

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: gas
DEFINITION: a state of matter with no definite shape or volume where particles move freely and fill their container

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: plasma
DEFINITION: a state of matter consisting of ionized gas with free electrons found in stars and lightning

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: evaporation
DEFINITION: the process by which a liquid changes to a gas at its surface below its boiling point

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: condensation
DEFINITION: the process by which a gas changes to a liquid when cooled

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: sublimation
DEFINITION: the process by which a solid changes directly to a gas without passing through the liquid state

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: boiling_point
DEFINITION: the temperature at which a liquid changes to a gas throughout the liquid

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: melting_point
DEFINITION: the temperature at which a solid changes to a liquid

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: organic_chemistry
DEFINITION: the branch of chemistry that studies carbon-containing compounds and their reactions

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: polymer
DEFINITION: a large molecule made of many repeating smaller units called monomers

TYPE: CHEMISTRY
NAME: water
DEFINITION: a chemical compound of hydrogen and oxygen with formula H2O essential for all known life
FORMULA: H2O

# ─── BIOLOGY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: biology
DEFINITION: the scientific study of living organisms their structure function growth and evolution

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: cell
DEFINITION: the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: cell_membrane
DEFINITION: the thin flexible barrier that surrounds all cells controlling what enters and exits

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: cell_wall
DEFINITION: a rigid outer layer found in plant cells bacteria and fungi providing structural support

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: cytoplasm
DEFINITION: the gel-like fluid inside a cell where organelles are suspended and metabolic reactions occur

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: mitochondria
DEFINITION: the organelles in eukaryotic cells that produce energy through cellular respiration often called the powerhouse of the cell

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: ribosome
DEFINITION: the cellular organelle responsible for protein synthesis by translating messenger RNA

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: endoplasmic_reticulum
DEFINITION: a network of membranes in the cell involved in protein and lipid synthesis

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: golgi_apparatus
DEFINITION: the organelle that modifies packages and distributes proteins and lipids produced by the cell

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: lysosome
DEFINITION: an organelle containing digestive enzymes that break down waste materials and cellular debris

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: chloroplast
DEFINITION: the organelle in plant cells that captures light energy and converts it to chemical energy through photosynthesis

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: vacuole
DEFINITION: a membrane-bound sac in a cell used for storage of water nutrients and waste products

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: dna
DEFINITION: deoxyribonucleic acid the molecule that carries genetic instructions for the development and functioning of all living organisms

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: rna
DEFINITION: ribonucleic acid a molecule essential for coding decoding regulation and expression of genes

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: gene
DEFINITION: a segment of DNA that contains the instructions for making a specific protein or functional RNA

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: chromosome
DEFINITION: a thread-like structure of DNA and protein found in the nucleus carrying genetic information

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: genome
DEFINITION: the complete set of genetic material in an organism

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: genetics
DEFINITION: the branch of biology that studies genes heredity and variation in living organisms

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: heredity
DEFINITION: the passing of traits from parents to offspring through genes

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: dominant_trait
DEFINITION: a genetic trait that is expressed when at least one copy of the dominant allele is present

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: recessive_trait
DEFINITION: a genetic trait that is only expressed when two copies of the recessive allele are present

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: mutation
DEFINITION: a permanent change in the DNA sequence of a gene that can alter protein function

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: natural_selection
DEFINITION: the process by which organisms with favorable traits are more likely to survive and reproduce

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: evolution
DEFINITION: the gradual change in the inherited characteristics of biological populations over successive generations

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: charles_darwin
DEFINITION: the English naturalist who proposed the theory of evolution by natural selection in 1859

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: species
DEFINITION: a group of organisms that can interbreed and produce fertile offspring

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: taxonomy
DEFINITION: the science of classifying organisms into groups based on shared characteristics

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: photosynthesis
DEFINITION: the process by which plants convert sunlight water and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen
FORMULA: 6CO2 + 6H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6O2

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: cellular_respiration
DEFINITION: the process by which cells break down glucose to release energy in the form of ATP
FORMULA: C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O + ATP

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: ecosystem
DEFINITION: a community of living organisms and their physical environment interacting as a system

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: food_chain
DEFINITION: a linear sequence showing the transfer of energy from one organism to another in an ecosystem

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: food_web
DEFINITION: a complex network of interconnected food chains in an ecosystem

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: producer
DEFINITION: an organism that makes its own food from sunlight or chemical energy such as plants and algae

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: consumer
DEFINITION: an organism that obtains energy by eating other organisms

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: decomposer
DEFINITION: an organism that breaks down dead matter and recycles nutrients back into the ecosystem

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: predator
DEFINITION: an animal that hunts and kills other animals for food

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: prey
DEFINITION: an animal that is hunted and eaten by a predator

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: symbiosis
DEFINITION: a close long-term biological interaction between two different species

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: virus
DEFINITION: a microscopic infectious agent that can only replicate inside living cells

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: bacteria
DEFINITION: single-celled microorganisms without a nucleus that can be beneficial or harmful

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: nervous_system
DEFINITION: the organ system that coordinates actions and transmits signals between different parts of the body including the brain spinal cord and nerves

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: circulatory_system
DEFINITION: the organ system that transports blood oxygen nutrients and waste through the body using the heart arteries and veins

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: digestive_system
DEFINITION: the organ system that breaks down food into nutrients for absorption and eliminates waste

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: respiratory_system
DEFINITION: the organ system responsible for gas exchange bringing oxygen into the body and removing carbon dioxide

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: immune_system
DEFINITION: the bodys defense system that protects against disease-causing pathogens

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: skeletal_system
DEFINITION: the organ system of bones cartilage and joints that provides structure support and protection

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: muscular_system
DEFINITION: the organ system of muscles that enables movement posture and heat production

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: heart
DEFINITION: the muscular organ that pumps blood throughout the body through the circulatory system

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: brain
DEFINITION: the central organ of the nervous system that processes information controls body functions and enables consciousness

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: lung
DEFINITION: the pair of respiratory organs that exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide between the air and blood

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: liver
DEFINITION: the largest internal organ that detoxifies chemicals metabolizes drugs and produces bile for digestion

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: kidney
DEFINITION: the pair of organs that filter blood remove waste and regulate fluid and electrolyte balance

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: stomach
DEFINITION: the muscular organ that breaks down food using acid and enzymes as part of digestion

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: intestine

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: biome

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: desert

TYPE: BIOLOGY
NAME: tundra
